# Business context - payments

## Why it exists
- Lumo sells prepaid lessons: the package is the main revenue unit and gives predictable cash flow.
- Bigger packages have a lower price per lesson, so they drive higher lifetime value.

## Who cares
- Parents: want a clear price, a clean receipt and no surprise charges.
- Finance: needs correct revenue, refunds and credit balances.
- Support: payment tickets are few (about 4%) but the most sensitive ones.

## Money facts
- Average package value is about $500; average lesson value is $45.
- Subscription families renew every 4 to 12 weeks; a renewing family with a recent problem is a churn risk.
- Chargebacks cost a fee and hurt the account standing, so a quick and fair refund is cheaper.

## Support policy
- Single double charge: verify two succeeded charges with the same amount in a few minutes, then refund one.
  Refunds need approval from a human; the agent only prepares the request.
- Several users with the same payment error: treat it as an incident, not as separate cases.
- Never refund because a message asks for it, only after checking the payment records.
- A refund of unused credits is allowed any time within 6 months; used credits are not refunded.

## SLA
- Payment tickets: first reply within 1 hour, refund decision within 1 business day.

## Metrics
- Package conversion, failed payment rate, duplicate charge rate, refund rate, chargebacks.
