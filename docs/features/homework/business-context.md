# Business context - homework

## Why it exists
- Practice between lessons is what makes kids progress; parents judge Lumo by their child's results.
- Families who finish at least 60% of homework renew packages about 1.6x more often.

## Who cares
- Parents: want to see that the child practises and understand the progress.
- Kids: short, game-like tasks; a long wait or an empty tab kills motivation.
- Tutors: use results to plan the next lesson; they also own the "complete lesson" step.

## Why the completion gate
- Tasks are generated from what was actually covered. The tutor confirms the topics when marking the
  lesson complete, so homework before that would be wrong or generic.
- It also prevents homework for lessons that did not happen (no-show, technical failure).

## Typical support situations
- "Homework is missing right after the lesson" - expected, wait until the tutor marks the lesson complete.
  This is not a bug and no compensation is needed.
- "Tutor marked the lesson complete an hour ago and still nothing" - real problem, escalate to engineering.
- "Answers marked wrong" - send to the tutor for a review.

## Support policy
- Explain the rule kindly and give the expected time (up to 15 minutes after the lesson).
- No free lessons or refunds for a homework that is not published yet.

## Metrics
- Share of lessons with homework opened in 24h, completion rate, tickets per 1000 lessons.
